FAQ

Questions

Does it really analyze my audio?

Yes, in your browser: duration, a tempo estimate from the beat's pulse, energy level and the waveform. Key and section detection need a server-side audio service, so they are marked as unavailable rather than guessed.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. Files are decoded locally and never leave your device in this build.

Can it write in Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa and Pidgin?

Yes. Choose a language or a mix, and use the slang and Pidgin sliders to tune how local it sounds.

What about PDF / DOCX export and accounts?

Those require a backend. This version exports TXT and a project file, and keeps projects privately in your own browser.
